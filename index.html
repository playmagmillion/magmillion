How it works

What is Magmillion?

Magmillion is a free daily word game where the rarest answer wins. Every day everyone gets the same seven prompts, like "Name a fruit" or "Name a knot." Common answers barely scratch the surface, and the ones nobody else thinks of send your drill past the mantle toward the core. After each prompt you see the rarest answers you missed, so you learn a new word or two every day.

If you like Krillion, Cosmillion, or Moonshot, Magmillion plays the same way, with a volcano and a drill instead of the ocean or space. More games like Krillion
